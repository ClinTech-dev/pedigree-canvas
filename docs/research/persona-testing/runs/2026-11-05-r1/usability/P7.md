## Bennett/NSGC issues seen
- **Test result off the symbol** (K7): "BRCA2" without "VUS" on the individual. Bennett expects the result annotated under the individual.
- **Sibship birth order:** older brother drawn to the right of the proband.
- **Twin "?"** placement sits on the descent line above the split rather than clearly between the twin lines. It reads correctly, but it is cramped.
- **SAB triangle size:** Bennett/the standards doc call for a *small* triangle. Here it is drawn in the same bounding box as the other symbols (about 34 pt in the PDF, the same as a square). Minor.
- **Stillbirth: correct.** It is a sex symbol (◇ for unknown sex) with a deceased slash, "SB" and "GA: 30 wk", not a triangle. The panel copy explains the SB-vs-triangle rule explicitly.
- **SAB: correct.** It is an open triangle with "SAB" and "GA: 10 wk".
- PDF export drops the generation numerals (I–IV) that the canvas shows, so individuals can't be referenced as "III-1" in a letter.

## Deviations (Playwright-driving issues, not app problems)
- The repo's `node_modules/playwright` expected a browser build that isn't installed (`chromium_headless_shell-1228`). The runner was restarted with the machine's global Playwright install (browsers 1243). There was no app impact.
- The zygosity `<select>` options were read through a locator on the select's option text instead of a visual open of the native dropdown. That is equivalent to what she would see.
- Hovering the unselected mother (step 7) didn't show the add bubbles. This may be an artefact of `mouse.move` jumping without intermediate pointer events, not an app bug. Unverified.
- PDF size and raster nature were confirmed with `pdfinfo`/`pdfimages`. Sofia would see the file size in her downloads or the record system's upload limit.
- An extra SVG export was made only to cross-check the extension symbols.
- KLM counts are approximate tallies from the step scripts, not instrumented.
