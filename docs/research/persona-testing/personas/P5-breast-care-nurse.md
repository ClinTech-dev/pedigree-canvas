# P5 · Breast care nurse taking short family histories

## Setting
- Breast care nurse in a public hospital breast clinic [A].
- Takes a quick 3-generation family history to decide whether to refer to
  genetics; perhaps five a week [E6] [A].

## Devices and IT limits
- Shared ward and clinic PCs, generic or personal login depending on the area [E20] [A].
- Only approved software; would never request a new tool herself [E18] [A].

## Current tools and workflow
- Ticks a family-history section of the referral form and sometimes sketches a
  small pedigree on the form, which gets scanned [E16] [A].
- Enters key relatives in the EHR's family-history fields [E15] [E25].

## Frustrations
1. Unsure how to draw anything beyond parents, siblings and children [E6] [A].
2. Referral forms go back and forth because the history was incomplete [A].

## Goals and what "better" means to them
- Speed over completeness: get to a "refer / don't refer" decision fast [E6].
- Something that guides her through the questions.

## Attitude to new tools and money
- Would use a tool only if it's on the hospital's approved list or the genetics
  service gives it to her [E18] [A].
- No budget [A].

## Standards literacy
- Low to medium. Knows squares, circles and shading; unsure about deceased,
  twins, half-siblings. Won't notice subtle symbol errors but will notice confusion.

## Usability behaviour
- Mouse only; doesn't know keyboard shortcuts and won't look for them [A].
- Shared PC, 1920 x 1080 [A].
- Patience budget: 5 minutes [A]. Gives up rather than reading help.

## What this persona cannot tell us
- How Australian breast clinics actually refer [E9].
- Whether nurses would be allowed to use a web tool.
