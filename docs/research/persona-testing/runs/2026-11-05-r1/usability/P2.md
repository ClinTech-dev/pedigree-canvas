## Bennett/NSGC issues seen
- **Generation numerals (I–IV) are missing from the PNG export** but present on the canvas. Bennett §10 expects Roman numerals on the left margin. A marker would deduct.
- **Birth order**: new siblings always go to the right. The wand put younger before older (son 11 left of daughter 14), against "left-to-right birth order" (Bennett §layout rule 2). For a student who "might trust the tool over his own memory", this teaches the wrong thing.
- **Genetic test result**: "BRCA2" on the symbol with "VUS" only in the Key does not follow the convention of writing the test and result beneath the individual.
- **Twin zygosity**: the quick-add picker offers only MZ/DZ. Picking DZ when unsure is a natural student shortcut, and it encodes a definite DZ claim. Unknown (“?”) is available but hidden in the panel.
- Age "60s" coerced to "60" turns an approximate age into a precise false one.
- Positives: deceased slash, proband arrow + P, quadrant shading with an auto Key, the single-apex twin with "?", and "d. 58" formatting are all correct.

## Extension task notes
Task: export an image for a slide and check it at full size.
- PNG at 3x is **crisp at 100%** (P2-7): clean lines, clear text. This beats the "blurry Progeny export" frustration.
- **Transparent background** (RGBA, alpha 0). On a white slide it's fine. On a dark PowerPoint theme the black lines and text would disappear, and there is no "white background" option in the Export dialog or the menu.
- **Shape**: 2207 x 2353 portrait, because the Key sits below-left of the drawing. Fitted to a 16:9 slide height, the pedigree uses under half the slide width and labels are about 10 px. Tom would have to crop or move the Key by hand.
- **No generation numerals** in the export (see Bennett issues). The file name is "Untitled Pedigree.png" because the document was never renamed. Nothing prompts for a title.
- The menu (☰) has a Legend, Theme and Preferences, but no export options (background, numerals, crop to content).

## Deviations
- Live site blocked; used the local production build of e1cb248 (as instructed).
- Driven by Playwright through a long-lived browser with step scripts. Hover buttons were clicked at coordinates read from screenshots. A human would see them on hover in the same way.
- The zygosity `<select>` options were read through Playwright (`allTextContents`) instead of a screenshot of the opened native dropdown. A human opening the dropdown sees the same three options.
- "Check at full size" was simulated by cropping the PNG at 100% and pasting it onto a 1280 x 720 white canvas to stand in for a slide. PowerPoint itself was not used.
- No tooltip could be confirmed on the wand button. A Playwright hover of about 1.2 s showed none. A real user's longer hover might reveal a native `title` tooltip that headless screenshots don't render.
- No app crashes or Playwright failures. All actions applied on the first try at the driving level.
