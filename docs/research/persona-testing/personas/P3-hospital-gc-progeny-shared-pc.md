# P3 · Senior hospital genetic counsellor, service runs Progeny, shared clinic PCs

## Setting
- Senior counsellor in a public hospital clinical genetics service, about 15
  years' experience; general and cancer clinics [E4] [A].
- Mix of in-person clinics and telehealth from hospital rooms [E3] [A].
- Reviews trainees' pedigrees and helps choose the service's tools [A].

## Devices and IT limits
- Hot-desks on shared clinic PCs; logs in as herself to Windows, but the browser
  profile and local storage may be shared or wiped at logout [E20] [A].
- Only approved software. Anything else is shadow IT and needs a request to
  digital health, about a week to assess [E18] [E19].
- Patient data must not leave the hospital network without approval [E18].

## Current tools and workflow
- The service licenses Progeny Clinical; pedigrees live in Progeny's database [E11].
- The hospital EHR has a family-history table nobody keeps up to date; a PDF of
  the pedigree is attached to the clinic letter [E15] [E25] [A].
- Retypes the family into CanRisk for cancer referrals [E23] [E24].

## Frustrations
1. Progeny is slow to open on shared PCs and needs a licence seat; seats run out [A].
2. Retyping into CanRisk and the EHR [E23].
3. Can't draft from home or before clinic without remote access [A].

## Goals and what "better" means to them
- Draft quickly anywhere, then get it into Progeny so the database stays the
  single source of truth [E28].
- Nothing that creates an IT incident or a privacy complaint.

## Attitude to new tools and money
- Cautious. Will try a tool personally only if it clearly keeps data local, and
  would need a privacy statement to show IT [E18] [E19] [E30].
- Has no budget authority; the service buys at contract level [E17] [E30].

## Standards literacy
- Very high. Will check consanguinity lines, twins, adoption and the legend.

## Usability behaviour
- Competent but busy; mouse-first, learns shortcuts slowly [A].
- Hospital monitor, 1920 x 1080, Edge [A].
- Patience budget: 10 minutes; if the first family doesn't work she stops [A].
- First question on opening: "where is this data stored?"

## What this persona cannot tell us
- Which import formats Progeny Clinical accepts in her service's configuration [E28].
- How her hospital's IT would actually rule on a local-first web app (H5).
- Whether local storage is wiped at logout on real hospital PCs (H5).
