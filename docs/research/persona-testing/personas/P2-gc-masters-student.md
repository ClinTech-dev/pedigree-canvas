# P2 · Second-year genetic counselling master's student

## Setting
- Second year of a two-year Master of Genetic Counselling [A].
- Clinical placements two days a week, rotating between a public hospital
  genetics service and a private prenatal clinic [A].
- Draws pedigrees for assessments, case presentations and placement logbooks [E5].

## Devices and IT limits
- Own Windows laptop, Chrome or Edge [A]. On placement he uses the hospital's
  shared PCs and is not allowed to install anything [E18] [A].
- University gives him Microsoft 365; assignments go in as Word or PDF [A].

## Current tools and workflow
- Learned to draw by hand first; uses the free Progeny online tool for
  assessments because the course mentioned it [E13] [E5].
- Exports an image and pastes it into Word or PowerPoint for case presentations [E8] [A].
- On placement, draws on paper; the supervisor redraws or scans it [E16] [A].

## Frustrations
1. Getting symbols exactly right for marking (twins, adoption, pregnancy loss) and
   losing marks for small errors [E5] [A].
2. The free tool's image export looks blurry when pasted into slides [A].
3. Re-drawing the same case several times as it's refined for a presentation [A].

## Goals and what "better" means to them
- A free tool that is clearly "correct" by Bennett/NSGC so he can trust it for marking.
- Crisp export into slides and Word.

## Attitude to new tools and money
- Will not pay; free wins [E5] [E29].
- Very influenced by what lecturers and classmates use; shares tools in the cohort
  group chat [E29] [A].

## Standards literacy
- Medium to high on paper, freshly taught, but unsure on edge cases. He might
  trust the tool over his own memory, so a tool error could teach him the wrong thing.

## Usability behaviour
- Digital native; explores menus and right-click freely; tries keyboard shortcuts [A].
- Laptop screen, 1366 x 768 [A].
- Patience budget: 20 minutes, more if it's for an assignment [A].

## What this persona cannot tell us
- What Australian programmes actually teach or require [E9].
- Whether educators would endorse a new tool.
