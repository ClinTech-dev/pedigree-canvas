# P8 · Clinical geneticist who reviews pedigrees (the sceptic)

## Setting
- Consultant clinical geneticist in a public tertiary genetics service [A].
- Reviews counsellors' pedigrees before results clinics; draws large or
  consanguineous families himself [E7].
- Sits on the group that would advise on a software tender [E17] [E30] [A].

## Devices and IT limits
- Hospital PC and a personal laptop; the hospital applies the usual approved-software rules [E18] [A].

## Current tools and workflow
- Uses Progeny through the service licence [E11].
- Annotates printed pedigrees by hand at case meetings [A].

## Frustrations
1. Large, multi-branch and consanguineous families lay out badly in most tools [E7].
2. Pedigrees from different counsellors look different; consistency matters [A].

## Goals and what "better" means to them
- Correct, readable layout for complicated families; nothing clinically misleading.
- Evidence the tool is safe if it is used in care [E21].

## Attitude to new tools and money
- Sceptical of new tools; asks who maintains it, where data goes, and what
  happens when it's wrong [E21] [E30] [A].
- Not a buyer himself, but his opinion can sink a tender [E30] [A].

## Standards literacy
- Expert. Notices layout crossings, consanguinity loops, generation numbering.

## Usability behaviour
- Tries to break it: builds a cousin marriage, a half-sibship, a multi-partner
  person [A].
- Desktop 1920 x 1080 [A].
- Patience budget: 10 minutes; makes a judgement in the first 3 [A].

## What this persona cannot tell us
- What his service's clinical safety officer would require [E21].
- Whether he'd recommend it to counsellors.
